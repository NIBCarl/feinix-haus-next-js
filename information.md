4. Current Funnel Process (The Problem)

Current Process:

1. Lead opts in via GHL for the "Done Waiting" challenge
2. GHL delivers the 5-day challenge via email automation
3. You manually collect their Gmail address
4. You manually add them to the Skool community

The Bottleneck:
Manual Gmail collection and manual Skool invites don't scale. GHL and Skool don't natively integrate for membership access.

The Solution (What We Designed):

· Keep the GHL funnel for lead capture, challenge delivery, and a low-ticket tripwire sale ($27 one-time digital product).
· The Skool community membership ($22/month, first week free) is purchased through Skool's own checkout—not GHL.
· The Thank-you Page (after tripwire buy or decline) invites them to click the Skool join link.
· Skool handles all billing, free trial, welcome DMs, and automatic member access.
· Zero manual work. Fully automated.
