## What do you do, and who do you serve?

Feinix Haus is a lifestyle wellness and fitness community for women after cancer think Peloton meets Soho House, built specifically for the woman the wellness world forgot about after treatment ended.

There are 4 million breast cancer survivors in the United States alone. When treatment ends the medical system hands them a finish line and sends them home. The wellness world wants them healing. The fitness world doesn't know they exist. The lifestyle brands have completely forgotten them. The woman in her After, 6 months, a year, three years post-treatment is invisible. She's not sick enough for the cancer community. She's not "back to normal" enough for everyone else. She exists in a gap nobody has built for.

Feinix Haus is a lifestyle, wellness and fitness membership community built specifically for post-cancer women who are done recovering and ready to actually live.

Not a support group. Not a coaching program. Not another heavy healing space. A lifestyle. A fitness community. A culture. The room she's been looking for.
